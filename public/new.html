"There is never a better time than now"

Your first night, step by step

No booking, no forms, no app. The bit most people are actually nervous about, in four moves.

1

Arrive 6.00pm

Fifteen minutes before the 6.15 session, on a Tuesday, Thursday or Sunday. We're inside Churchills Pro Boxing Gym — look for the Churchills sign, not ours.

2

Say it's your first

Tell any coach on the floor. They'll take your name, ask about injuries, and put you where you need to be. You won't be left to work it out.

3

Pay £9

On the night, no sign-up, no minimum. If you decide it's not for you, that's where it ends — nothing to cancel.

4

Wrap up and go

Borrow gloves from the club. Wraps are for sale at the door if you don't have a pair. Then it's skipping, shadow, bags and pads for 45 minutes.

Keep it simple

What to bring

You don't need kit to start — everything optional can be borrowed or bought at the door.

Bring with you

  • Training clothesAnything you can sweat in.
  • TrainersOr boxing boots if you already own a pair.
  • Water
  • Hand wrapsOptional first time — buy a set at the club.
  • Skipping ropeOptional — but get one, you'll use it every session.
  • GlovesOptional — borrow a pair from us until you buy your own.

Nobody's turned away for the wrong shoes

Everything on that list that's dashed, we can lend you or sell you at the door for less than a pair of gloves online.

Buy gear once you know you're staying — not before. Ask a coach what's worth buying first and they'll tell you straight.

Getting carded

Competing for Rebels

Stepping into the ring in front of a crowd changes you. We'll get you there.

When you're ready to fight, we register you with England Boxing as an amateur. That's what "getting carded" means — every amateur boxer carries a record card, and we'll walk you through the paperwork.

Already carded elsewhere? Speak to a coach about transferring to Rebels.

Our coaches have taken boxers from novice to national championships and international competition. Danny Shannon is a Level 2 and BBBofC pro coach; Julia Lee is a professional boxer and three-time national champion.

We're always looking for fighters to represent Rebels.
